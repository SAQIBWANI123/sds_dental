Real Clinic Process

How It Works

The module follows the real visit from booking to follow-up.

1

Register

Add the patient with history, allergies and insurance.		 2

Book

Choose dentist, chair and time. Patient is emailed.		 3

Check In

Move the visit through Checked In and In Progress.		 4

Plan

Propose a phased plan with an estimate.
5

Treat

Record procedures per tooth; the chart updates.		 6

Prescribe

Print the prescription and send lab work.		 7

Invoice

Create the invoice and collect payment.		 8

Review

Follow results on the dashboard.

Screenshots

Module Screenshots

The screens your team will use every day.

Dashboard

Clinic Dashboard

Eight KPI cards show patients, appointments today, the next seven days, open treatments, revenue, outstanding invoices, open plans and lab orders waiting. Today's schedule, revenue, status mix and dentist workload sit below.

Patients

Patient Record & Dental Chart

One record per patient with contact details, medical history, insurance and smart buttons. Allergies show in a warning banner, while all 32 teeth use FDI notation and colour by latest condition.

Scheduling

Appointment Calendar

A week calendar with a colour for each status. Open any booking with one click. The same data is also available as list, kanban, pivot and graph views.

Scheduling

Appointment Workflow

Move each visit through Draft, Confirmed, Checked In, In Progress and Done. Overlapping bookings for the same dentist or chair are blocked, and confirming emails the patient.

Clinical

Treatment & Invoicing

Record procedure, tooth, surface, quantity, price and discount. Set Chart Status After and the chart updates on completion. Create Invoice makes the customer invoice in one click.

Clinical

Treatment Plans

Plan bigger cases in four phases with a total estimate. When the patient accepts, Start Treatment copies every line into a new treatment.

Clinical

Prescription PDF

Pick medicines with default dosage, then print a clean PDF with diagnosis, allergy warning and a signature line.

Clinical

Dental Lab Orders

Track crowns, bridges, dentures and aligners from Draft to Sent, Received and Fitted, with shade, due date and cost.

Setup

Procedure Catalogue

Keep one price list with code, category, duration and price. Sample procedures, medicines and chairs are installed with the module.

Security

User Roles

Assign one role per user. Higher roles include the lower ones.

DentistEverything above plus charts, treatments, plans, prescriptions, X-rays and lab orders.
ManagerFull access and the Configuration menu for dentists, procedures, medicines and chairs.
